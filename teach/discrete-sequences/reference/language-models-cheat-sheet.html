Bigram estimate

\(P(s_t \mid s_{t-1}) = \dfrac{\mathrm{Count}(s_{t-1}, s_t)}{\mathrm{Count}(s_{t-1})}\)

Bout grain: \(s_t\) = syllable id at bout t.

Perplexity (intuition)

\(\mathrm{PP} = 2^{H}\) or \(\exp(-\frac{1}{N}\sum \log P)\) on test transitions. Lower = more predictable bigrams. Compare before/after merge or between strata — not an absolute “good” threshold.
