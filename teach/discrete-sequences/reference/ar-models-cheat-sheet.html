Core equations

AR(p) \(Y_t = \phi_1 Y_{t-1} + \cdots + \phi_p Y_{t-p} + \varepsilon_t\),   \(\varepsilon_t \sim \text{WN}(0, \sigma^2)\)
AR(1) stationarity \(|\phi_1| < 1\)

Symbols

SymbolMeaning
\(Y_t\)Observation (or latent value) at time \(t\)
\(p\)Order — number of lags
\(\phi_k\)Coefficient on lag \(k\)
\(\varepsilon_t\)White-noise innovation at \(t\)
\(\phi\)AR(1) single coefficient (special case)
